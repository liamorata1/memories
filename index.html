01 · from my heart

A letter for you

There are things I wish I had handled better. This is me trying to say them honestly.

Hi, love.

Hindi ko alam kung paano mo matatanggap itong ginawa ko, or kung gugustuhin mo pa bang basahin. Pero gusto ko pa ring sabihin yung mga bagay na matagal ko nang gustong sabihin sa'yo.

I'm sorry. Alam kong nasaktan kita at nabigyan kita ng dahilan para magduda sa akin. Alam kong hindi sapat ang pagsabi ng sorry para maibalik agad yung tiwala at peace of mind na nawala sa'yo. Hindi ko rin gustong i-minimize yung naramdaman mo.

You mattered to me then, and you still matter to me now. Hindi naging laro para sa akin yung nararamdaman ko sa'yo. Pero naiintindihan ko na kahit genuine ang feelings ko, hindi ibig sabihin noon na hindi na kita kayang masaktan.

I can't undo what happened, and I can't ask you to pretend that it never happened. Ang kaya ko lang gawin ay aminin yung pagkakamali ko, matuto mula rito, at baguhin yung mga bagay na kailangan kong baguhin sa sarili ko.

Hindi ko ito ginagawa para ma-guilty ka o para maramdaman mong kailangan mo akong bigyan ng sagot. Gusto ko lang malaman mo na mahalaga sa akin yung naramdaman mo, pati yung sakit na naidulot ko sa'yo.

Thank you for the moments you shared with me, for the times you made me smile, and for letting me know a part of you. I will always be grateful that our paths crossed.

I'm sorry that I didn't take better care of what we had. I wish I had done some things differently, especially when it mattered most.

With all my sincerity,

the girl who is still learning to do better ♡

02 · once upon a time

How our story began

A little reminder of the story that became important to me.

When my world felt messy

We met at a time when my world felt like it was falling apart, when I felt like I had no one to turn to. Back then, I had no idea that meeting you would become one of the most meaningful parts of my life. I never expected that someone I met during one of my hardest times would become someone I would grow so attached to, someone I would come to miss this much, and someone I would wish I had loved a little better.

When talking to you felt different

Little by little, I got to know you. Our simple conversations became little reasons for me to smile, and there were moments when I found myself wanting to tell you even the smallest things about my day. Somehow, the simplest things felt more meaningful when I got to share them with you. Without even realizing it, you became someone I wanted to talk to, someone I wanted to share my little moments with, and someone who made my ordinary days feel a little less ordinary.

When you became someone special

I didn't even realize when you became part of my everyday life. Our simple conversations, the way I found myself worrying about you, and how I always wanted to spend time with you, even in the smallest ways, slowly became things I looked forward to. You became part of my routine without me even noticing, and somewhere along the way, talking to you stopped being just another part of my day. It became one of the parts I cherished the most.

When I realized love needs more than feelings

I've learned that a relationship isn't just about love. It also takes honesty, consistency, respect, accountability, and the willingness to truly listen to each other. Love may be what brings two people together, but it's the way they treat each other, the choices they make, and the effort they put in that help them grow together. And now, I understand that having genuine feelings for someone doesn't always mean you know how to love them the way they deserve. Sometimes, you have to learn, reflect, and become better, especially when your actions have hurt someone you never wanted to lose.

Where I am now

I can't change the past, but I can take responsibility for my part in what happened. I want to become someone who understands that my actions have consequences, not just someone who focuses on what I meant or intended. I'm learning that even when I never meant to hurt someone, the pain I caused is still real. And if I truly want to become better, I have to be willing to acknowledge my mistakes, learn from them, and show through my actions that I'm changing, rather than expecting my intentions alone to make things right.

04 · things I need to learn

My growth journal

I don't want to promise that I'll suddenly become perfect. I want to be honest about the work I need to do.

Taking responsibility

I need to learn to own my choices without making excuses or focusing only on what I intended. My intentions don't erase the effect of my actions on someone I care about.

Being honest and consistent

Trust is not something I can demand. It is built through repeated honest choices, keeping my word, and being consistent even when it's difficult or uncomfortable.

Understanding your feelings

I want to listen without immediately defending myself. I need to make room for your feelings, even when they are painful for me to hear, and respect the boundaries you set.

Changing beyond an apology

I know that a long message or a thoughtful website cannot prove that I have changed. That takes time, self-awareness, and actual decisions that match what I say.

Respecting your space

Loving someone also means respecting their choice, even when that choice hurts. I don't want my feelings to become a reason for you to feel pressured or responsible for me.

05 · one thing I still hope for

If you give me one last chance

This is not a demand or an expectation. Just an honest hope that I want to express to you.

If you give me one last chance, love...

I'm not asking you to forget what happened right away, to go back to the way things were as if no one ever got hurt, or to trust me again just because I've apologized. I know that the things that have been broken aren't so easily put back together.

If you give me one last chance, I want to earn your trust again, slowly and honestly. I don't want my words to be the only thing you hear from me. I want to learn how to be more careful with your feelings, how to be honest with you, and how to stand by the things I say.

I can't promise that I'll be perfect because I know there are still things I need to learn. But I want to take accountability when I make mistakes, listen when I've hurt you, and make real changes even when there's no guarantee that you'll let me back into your life.

I miss you. I miss talking to you and sharing the little things with you. But I don't want to use how much I miss you as a reason to pressure you into coming back. Your feelings matter, and so do your peace of mind and your decision.

If there's still even a little room in your heart to consider us again, I would be grateful for the opportunity to start slowly, without rushing you. We don't have to pretend that nothing happened. We can be honest about what hurt us and what needs to change.

And if you no longer feel the same way, I know I have to respect that, too. I won't tell you that you owe me another chance just because I love you or because I'm sorry. You're allowed to choose what's right for you.

I just want you to know that what I felt for you was real. I'm sorry for the ways I hurt you, and I'm sorry I didn't take better care of what we had when I had the chance.

I can't promise you a perfect future. All I can do is be honest about my hopes, take responsibility for my past, and work on becoming a better person from this point forward.

If there's still a chance for us,
I would want to do things differently this time. ♡
